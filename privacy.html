Privacy Policy

Channel Command · last updated 5 August 2026
In short: Channel Command is a private analytics dashboard used by its owner to view statistics for their own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it does not sell, share, or advertise with anyone's data.

Who this applies to

Channel Command is a personal tool operated by an individual creator for their own channels and those of a family member who has explicitly authorised it. Access is limited to those accounts; no one else can sign in or view the data.

What information the app accesses

When you sign in, the app reads only the following, and only for the account that signed in:

  • Profile basics — display name, avatar, username and account identifier.
  • Account statistics — follower/subscriber count, total likes and video count.
  • Your own videos and their public statistics — captions/titles, thumbnails, post dates, and counts of views, likes, comments and shares.
  • Analytics reports for your own content (YouTube only) — such as watch time, retention and traffic sources, as provided by the platform to the account owner.

What the app never does

  • It never reads or stores your password. Sign-in is handled entirely by Google or TikTok, and the app only receives a temporary access token.
  • It never posts, uploads, edits or deletes content on your behalf.
  • It never accesses your private messages, contacts, or files.
  • It never sells, rents, or shares your data with third parties.
  • It contains no advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

Where information is stored

  • On your device. Most data is cached in your browser's local storage so the dashboard loads quickly. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser data, or by using the app's Export/Import controls.
  • On a private server. To record statistics over time (and to keep several of the owner's devices in sync), public video statistics are stored in a private Cloudflare Workers KV namespace controlled solely by the app's owner. This is limited to the accounts described above, and access requires being signed in as the owner of that account.
  • Access tokens. Sign-in tokens are short-lived. Where a refresh token is used to keep recording statistics in the background, it is held as an encrypted server-side secret and is never exposed in the web page.

Retention

Recorded statistics are kept only as long as they are useful for tracking channel performance and are pruned automatically over time. You can request deletion of all stored data at any time using the contact address below, and it will be removed.

Revoking access

You can disconnect the app at any time, which immediately stops all access:

  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions

Children

This tool is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age required by the relevant platform's own terms.
