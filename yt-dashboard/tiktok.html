Sign in with TikTok to see your live stats. Your login is handled entirely by TikTok — this dashboard never sees your password or email, and it can only read your own profile and video statistics. It can never post, edit or delete anything.

The Worker holds the TikTok app secret and does the sign-in exchange, so nothing sensitive is ever in this page. Setup steps live in the repo's docs/tiktok-setup.md.
