Connect TikTok

Sign in with TikTok to see your live stats. Your login is handled entirely by TikTok — this dashboard never sees your password or email, and it can only read your own profile and video statistics. It can never post, edit or delete anything.

Your own server (the Worker) handles the sign-in and keeps TikTok’s app key, so nothing secret is ever stored in this page. Setup steps are in the repo's docs/tiktok-setup.md.

+Since refresh = views each post gained since the previous refresh. +This visit = views since you opened this page (a reload within 30 minutes carries on; after that it starts again from zero). ⚡ = speeding up: at least 1.5× as many views as the 24 hours before, and 50+ views in the last 24 hours. TikTok only shares views, likes, comments and shares with apps like this — not watch time, where viewers came from or who they are (only TikTok's API for Business carries those, and only for Business accounts). Only your newest 60 posts are fetched; an older post stops being counted rather than counting backwards.
